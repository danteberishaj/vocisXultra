# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Concert-goers in Kosovo and the diaspora** — checking who the choir is, what's next, when and where. Often on a phone, often in the evening.
- **Cultural partners, festivals, embassies, churches** — evaluating the foundation as a credible collaborator; reading mission, management, repertoire on desktop in daylight.
- **Prospective singers** — assessing whether this ensemble is serious and whether they belong in it.
- **Donors / supporters** — deciding whether this institution deserves trust.

Job to be done: understand in one visit that vocisXultra is a serious, internationally-minded choral institution — then follow it, book it, or join it.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for the vocisXultra Foundation (est. 2026, Prishtina) and its resident chamber choir, the vocisXultra Vocal Ensemble. One long scrolling page: Welcome/News → Foundation (mission, values, management) → Ensemble (identity, philosophy, repertoire, direction) → Events (upcoming, past, booking) → Contact & Impressum. Trilingual: English, Albanian (sq), German (de). Success: the page carries the authority of an institution that has existed for decades, though it is one year old.

## Brand Personality

**Bright, contemporary, resonant.** The name means *voices from beyond* — the page should feel like a modern hall on the morning of a rehearsal: white air, daylight, quiet confidence. Contemporary European cultural-institution register (a Kulturstiftung website, not a chapel and not a startup) — and **strictly not religious**: this is a secular arts institution (sacred repertoire is a genre it performs, not an identity). No candles, parchment, darkness-as-mood, engraving or liturgical styling anywhere — including imagery. The emotion to evoke: openness, clarity, anticipation.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing grammar: gradient blobs, hero-metric stats, identical icon-card grids, uppercase tracked eyebrows over every section, "🚀 Get Started" energy.
- The cream/parchment "classical music programme" cliché — warm-beige body backgrounds with a burgundy serif. The warmth belongs to light and type, not the surface.
- Concert-hall stock templates (red velvet, spotlight cones, golden bokeh).
- Anything that reads "AI made that": uniform section fade-ups, glassmorphism as decoration, gradient text.

## Design Principles

1. **Morning rehearsal light** — whiteness and air do the work of ornament; the page feels open, never dim.
2. **One blue on white** — pure white canvas, one harbor-blue accent; the palette is a single note held cleanly.
3. **One family, many weights** — Inter everywhere (user decision); hierarchy through weight, size and italics, not font changes. One wordmark gesture: the ✕ in vocis✕ultra.
4. **Content is the concert programme** — this is a content-heavy page; hierarchy, rhythm and pacing must make long reading feel curated, never dumped.
5. **Six centuries in one scroll** — historical depth (Renaissance → contemporary) is a real, ordered sequence; let chronology structure the repertoire, and nothing else be numbered.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum: body text ≥ 4.5:1 on the dark surface, muted text ≥ 3.5:1 (checked, not assumed).
- Full keyboard navigation, visible focus rings, skip link, semantic heading order.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere; reveals enhance already-visible content.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px; body ≥ 16px on mobile; no horizontal scroll at 375px.
- `lang` correct per locale (en / sq / de) so screen readers pronounce Albanian and German correctly.
